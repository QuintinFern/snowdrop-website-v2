The Essentials

Creating lasting change requires more than just a quick meal. Snowdrop United is proud to provide lightly used clothing alongside vital blankets and hygiene products. We provide everything from deodorant to toothbrushes and period products to ensure basic human needs are met.

Thanks to our partners at Security Gate and the George Ranch High School Student Council, we have successfully distributed large quantities of essential goods to the local community.
