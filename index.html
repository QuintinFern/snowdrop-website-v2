Mission Statement

Snowdrop United delivers meals, clothing, and hygiene essentials directly to Houston’s homeless community—no middleman at any point. From blankets on cold nights to cold water in summer heat, we meet urgent needs and work toward safer, more stable lives for low-income and homeless neighbors across Houston and beyond. As we grow, we plan educational seminars to help people build a strong foundation for success.
