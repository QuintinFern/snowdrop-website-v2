Our Mission

Snowdrop United provides the essentials to individuals in need. Our meal packages, clothing hand-outs, and hygiene packs go directly to the Houston area homeless population—no middle man at any point. From warm blankets for cold winter nights to icy water bottles for sweltering summer heat, we are determined to help lift individuals out of poverty and facilitate stable environments. As we expand, we plan to host educational seminars dedicated to giving everyone a strong foundation for a successful life.
